BananaPad third-party notices
=============================

BananaPad is an independent, unofficial native static-recompilation frontend.
It does not contain a game ROM. The user must supply a legally obtained,
supported Donkey Kong 64 ROM. Donkey Kong 64 and related names, copyrights,
and trademarks belong to their respective owners. BananaPad is not affiliated
